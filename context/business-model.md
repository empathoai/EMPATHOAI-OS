# Business Model

## Core problem hypothesis

Companies often lose growth not because they lack traffic, but because of weak positioning, conversion friction, unclear offers, fragmented systems, and execution misalignment.

## Offer sequence

```text
Precision Diagnostic
-> Growth Friction Audit
-> Growth implementation / retainer
```

- **Precision Diagnostic:** general EmpathoAI product.
- **Growth Friction Audit:** healthcare-aesthetics adaptation.
- **Implementation / retainer:** follow-on work after validated diagnostic findings.

The core diagnostic logic is:

```text
Signal -> Friction -> Cost -> Priority -> Action
```

## Commercial pipeline

```text
Identified -> Researched -> Contacted -> Engaged -> Meeting booked
-> Discovery completed -> Proposal sent -> Negotiation -> Won / Lost / Nurture
```

Stages are explicit and mutually exclusive. Required data must be complete before advancement.

## Current constraints and gates

- The business is currently operated by one person.
- The first commercial process is manual and tool-independent.
- GoHighLevel is deferred until the manual process is validated.
- A $1,500/month retainer target is provisional and must be tested against scope, margin, capacity, and client responsibilities.
- Projects depending on content require the client's willingness to record and provide video.
- Direct competitors of current clients are avoided as an ethical rule.
- Compliance is a differentiator in healthcare aesthetics.

## Required validation

A pilot must test the complete path from prospect research through contact, meeting, discovery, scope, estimate, pricing, proposal, contract, close/loss reason, and onboarding. Failures must be recorded with stage, root cause, impact, correction, owner, and retest date.
