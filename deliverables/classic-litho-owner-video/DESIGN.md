# DESIGN — Classic Litho Owner Video

## Style prompt
Cinematic industrial editorial. IBM Carbon precision meets premium print craft and restrained Apple-style industrial film language. The video must feel like it understands a real production company, not like a SaaS advertisement.

## Colors
- Canvas: `#0A0A0B` Obsidian Dark
- Deep surface: `#141416`
- Mid surface: `#1E1E22`
- Structural hairline: `#2D2D2F`
- Technical ivory: `#F5F5F5`
- Muted telemetry: `#8E8E93`
- Intervention signal: `#FF4402` — restricted to decision points, markers, and the EmpathoAI anchor.

## Typography
- Primary: IBM Plex Sans
- Telemetry / labels: IBM Plex Mono
- Headlines: bold, large, uppercase where used; no gradient text.

## Composition rules
- 16:9, 1920×1080, 24fps.
- Use asymmetric editorial panels, a technical grid, sharp 0px corners, and generous negative space.
- Use three visual layers: textured/grid background, real Classic Litho portfolio imagery, and foreground typography/telemetry.
- Use source imagery as moving editorial plates; no stock photographs or fabricated dashboards.

## Motion
- Mechanical, controlled, and deterministic: shutter reveals, grid locks, focus pulls, slow image drift, and limited signal-line routing.
- Each scene has a distinct entrance and a restrained ambient movement.
- Transitions are clean editorial handoffs, never glitchy.

## What not to do
- No generic AI iconography, neon gradients, rounded SaaS cards, faux software UI, or stock office imagery.
- Do not imply that client logos endorse EmpathoAI.
- Do not assert non-public Classic Litho conditions, results, or diagnostics.
- Do not quote pricing, retainers, or offer scope.
