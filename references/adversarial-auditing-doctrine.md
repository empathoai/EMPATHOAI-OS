# Operational Reference: Adversarial Auditing & Prompt Neutrality Doctrine

## Context & Provenance
Extracted and validated during the commercial master system certification with Alex Guajardo and Sally (NotebookLM) on 2026-09-17.

---

## 1. The Zero-Complacency Mandate (Adversarial Stress-Testing)
* **The Danger:** A single prompt or single evaluation pass always defaults to superficial consensus, polite praise, and hidden operational leaks.
* **The Standard:** 
  1. Reject early affirmative responses that lack granular line-by-line evidence.
  2. Specifically challenge the evaluator: *"Identify all friction points, unclosed loops, and operational leaks with zero complacency."*
  3. Iterate continuously until the system is certified as 100% hermetic with zero remaining observations.

---

## 2. Prompt Neutrality (Preventing Oracle Contamination)
* **The Danger:** Introducing leading assumptions, patch terminology, or author name-dropping contaminates the knowledge engine. The evaluator begins debating the prompt rather than auditing the source text against literature ground truth.
* **The Protocol:**
  * Clean, blind, and neutral prompts only:
    > *"Audit this document end-to-end against your knowledge base with zero complacency and issue your clinical verdict on whether it is 100% hermetic."*
  * Never state: *"Here are the 5 fixes we made, tell me if they are good."*
  * Never force jargon from outside frameworks into the prompt.

---

## 3. The Boundary Condition Filter (The Anti-Pattern Test)
* **The Fallacy:** Believing a certified system applies universally to all niches.
* **The Protocol:**
  * Every completed framework must answer:
    1. Where is this system 100% lethal? (Native ICP: High-Ticket B2B, High-LTV Consultative B2C, Multi-Unit physical operators).
    2. Where does this system break and destroy margins? (Anti-patterns: Low-Ticket B2C e-commerce, Government tenders/B2G, pure commodities, micro-SaaS).
    3. What components are universal (psychological principles) vs adaptable (metrics, registries, scripts)?
