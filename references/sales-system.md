2. **[Playbook 02: Surgical Outreach, Gatekeeper Bypass & 10-Minute Diagnostic Scheduling](file:///F:/EmpathoKnowledge/frameworks/playbook-comercial-02-surgical-outreach-and-gatekeeper.md)**
   - **The 120-Second Pre-Call Reset Routine:** 3-step psychological calibration (Visualization, Ego-Detachment, Doctor of Sales Authority Posture).

   - 30-Second Pattern-Interrupt Telephone Aperture with UDM (selling solely the 10-minute diagnostic session).

3. **[Playbook 03: Discovery Call Script & Clinical Diagnostic Questioning](file:///F:/EmpathoKnowledge/frameworks/playbook-comercial-03-discovery-call-script.md)**
   - **The Decision-Maker Innegotiability Protocol:** Minute-0 verification check & cold reschedule script if any partner/CFO is absent.
   - Framing and agenda control using the **Agenda Close** with **Referral Pre-Framing Seed** (minutes 0 to 3).
   - **The Minute-8 Micro-Renegotiation Protocol:** Explicit time extension check bridging the 10-minute outreach hook to the 30-minute diagnostic session.
   - Clinical SPIN Question Battery with **The Neil Rackham Gate:** Prohibited from presenting proposals on Implicit Needs; must isolate the **Explicit Need**.
   - Isolating the **Dominant Buying Motive (DBM)**: Operational Relief, Reputation Protection, or Hard ROI.
   - Clinical **Check Close** diagnostic validation and Decision-Maker Lock.

4. **[Playbook 04: Commercial Proposal Architecture & Logical Selling](file:///F:/EmpathoKnowledge/frameworks/playbook-comercial-04-proposal-and-closing.md)**
   - **Ironclad Decision-Maker Lock at Minute 0:** Prohibited from presenting slides, figures, or proposals if any equity partner/CFO is absent; cold reschedule mandate.
   - 6-Visual-Block presentation structure with **Referral Commitment Clause** in Block 6.
   - Logical Selling (10:1 ROI return anchoring vs validated annual COI).
   - Two-tier offer architecture: Bespoke Architecture Setup (one-time) + Ongoing Optimization Retainer (monthly).
   - Price resistance neutralizers: Daily cost reduction ($20/day) and Customer Unit Equivalency (1 client covers retainer).
   - **The Prohibited Vocabulary Doctrine:** Eliminating fear triggers (*contract, price, fee, sign, buy*) and substituting executive terms (*agreement, total investment, monthly investment, approval, deploy*).
   - The Moment of Truth and strict **Strategic Silence Protocol** (10 to 45 seconds of calm, uninterrupted pause).
   - High-authority closing battery: **Order Blank Close (Hoja de Pedido)**, Alternative Close, Assumptive Close, Sharp Angle / Suppose Close, and Sudden Death Close (Elegant Ultimatum).

5. **[Playbook 05: Live Objection Handling Matrix, Value Follow-Up & Break-Up Protocol](file:///F:/EmpathoKnowledge/frameworks/playbook-comercial-05-objection-and-closing.md)**
   - **Clinical Discrimination:** Real Condition (close file) vs. Smokescreen Objection.
   - **Mandatory Pre-Closing Filters:** Filter 1 (Remaining Objections Close) & Filter 2 (Sharp Angle Sincerity Test).
   - 6-step clinical objection sequence with universal **Emotional Cushion** (*Feel, Felt, Found*).
   - 4 verbatim combat battlecards (Money/Budget, Partner/Board, Time/Procrastination, Competition/In-House AI).
   - **The Doorknob / Columbo Close Protocol (Cierre del Picaporte):** Conceding, walking to the door, turning around, and extracting the genuine blocker.
   - **The Dual-Track Value Follow-Up Cadence:** 7-touch value architecture with **Accelerated Day-14 Break-Up** for unresponsive ghosting leads vs. 30-day collaborative evaluation.

6. **[Playbook 06: Financial Settlement, Rapid Onboarding & Anti-Buyer's Remorse Shield](file:///F:/EmpathoKnowledge/frameworks/playbook-comercial-06-cash-collection-and-onboarding.md)**
   - Zero Disconnection Mandate: Processing and verifying Setup payment live on the closing call without hanging up.
   - **The Dual-Rail Cash Collection Protocol:**
     - *Rail 1 (SMB / Clinics < $5,000 USD):* Mandatory in-session corporate credit/debit card processing via encrypted Stripe gateway.
     - *Rail 2 (Enterprise > $5,000 USD):* Live Digital Agreement Signature on screen + Corporate Invoice with verified scheduled wire / PO authorization on screen before releasing Kickoff.
   - Deflecting the "I'll pay later by email" dodge by reframing live payment as Step 1 to lock the engineering window.
   - The 2-Hour Anti-Buyer's Remorse Shield: Executive authority video/message sent to UDM to eliminate 24-48h cognitive dissonance and third-party sabotage.
   - 24-Hour Rapid Onboarding: 4-item, 3-minute intake form and locking the 20-minute Technical Kickoff session on the calendar before concluding the call.
