## 02 — MASTER LOGO & LEAD ANCHOR SYSTEM

```text
       ■ EMPATHOAI                            ┌────────┐
                                              │  ┌───  │
   [Primary Wordmark with Lead Anchor]        │  └───■ │
                                              └────────┘
                                           [E Utility Mark]
```

### 2.1 Primary Master Wordmark (Lead Anchor System)
* **Architecture:** Monolithic typography preceded by the **Precision Lead Anchor Block (`■`)**.
* **Neuromarketing Rationale (Klarić / Braidot / Cialdini):** The square on the left acts as an involuntary foveal trigger (orientation reflex in $<50\text{ ms}$) that launches the eye into the reading path of the brand name and channels attention back to the presenter.
* **Typography:** IBM Plex Sans Bold (`700`), uppercase, tracking `0.14em`.
* **Color Distribution:**
  * `■` (Lead Precision Anchor Block) $\rightarrow$ Electric Signal Orange (`#FF4402`), perfect 1:1 mathematical square ($0\text{px}$ radius).
  * `EMPATHOAI` $\rightarrow$ Technical Ivory (`#F5F5F5` on dark canvas) / Deep Graphite (`#0A0A0B` on light canvas).
* **Prohibitions:** Never split the wordmark by coloring "AI" in orange. No gradients, no drop shadows, no 3D bevels, no rounded corners.

### 2.2 Micro-Mark / "E" Utility Mark
* **Geometry:** Monolithic `E` derived from IBM Plex geometry with a **1:1 square anchor ($64\times64\text{ px}$)** in Electric Signal Orange (`#FF4402`) on the lower right terminal.
* **Canvas:** Obsidian Dark (`#0A0A0B`) with 1px Technical Hairline border (`#2D2D2F`).
* **Roles:** Favicon, Social Avatars (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, WhatsApp), Document certification stamp.
* **Absolute Chromatic Invariance Rule (PROHIBICIÓN DE MONOCROMÍA):**
  * El Micro-Mark / Isotipo tiene **prohibición estricta de renderizado monocromático** (100% blanco o 100% negro).
  * *Razón Neuro-Arquitectónica:* Si el ancla terminal se tiñe del mismo color que el cuerpo de la `E`, el ancla foveal colapsa y el identificador se degrada a una letra genérica. El isotipo **siempre** exige el ancla activa en `#FF4402` sobre lienzo `#0A0A0B`.
  * *Aplicaciones de Tinta Única:* Si un medio físico requiere estrictamente 1 sola tinta (ej. grabado láser ciego, sello seco térmico, impresión a 1 tinta), debe utilizarse el **Master Wordmark Monocromático** (`■ EMPATHOAI`), jamás el Isotipo aislado.

## 04 — TYPOGRAPHY & DATA HIERARCHY

* **Primary Editorial Font:** `IBM Plex Sans` (Light, Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold).
* **Data, Telemetry & Code Font:** `IBM Plex Mono` (Regular, Medium, SemiBold).

### 4.1 Type Scale
* **Display H1 (Hero & Document Covers):** IBM Plex Sans Bold, `48px – 64px`, Line Height `1.05`, Letter Spacing `-0.02em`.
* **Section H2:** IBM Plex Sans SemiBold, `32px – 40px`, Line Height `1.15`, Letter Spacing `-0.01em`.
* **Subhead H3:** IBM Plex Sans Medium, `20px – 24px`, Line Height `1.30`, Letter Spacing `0.00em`.
* **Body Text:** IBM Plex Sans Regular, `15px – 17px`, Line Height `1.60`, Color `#F5F5F5` or `#C7C7CC`.
* **Telemetry / Metrics:** IBM Plex Mono Medium, `12px – 14px`, Letter Spacing `0.05em`, Color `#8E8E93` or `#FF4402`.
